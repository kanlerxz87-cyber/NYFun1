Activities

Six attractions with hours and admission read from their own sites, plus five ideas that belong on the shortlist but were not verified in this pass. Every closure and exception is listed, because in February a closed door costs you a whole afternoon.

February is the trade-off. The High Line, Top of the Rock and the Statue of Liberty ferry are all outdoors and all cheaper and emptier in your window than in spring. The Met, MoMA and The Modern are the indoor anchors that make a cold week work — and The Modern is inside MoMA, which is why that pairing is in the itinerary.

The Metropolitan Museum of Art (Fifth Avenue) hours unverified

1000 Fifth Avenue, New York, NY 10028

Hours: hours not published
Admission: Adults $30 · Seniors (65+) $22 · Students $17 · Children 12 & under free · NY/NJ/CT students and NY residents pay-what-you-wish

Getting there: 4/5/6 to 86th St then 3 blocks west (~10 min walk, half a mile); M1–M4 buses along Fifth/Madison to 82nd/83rd

Booking: https://engage.metmuseum.org/admission

Closures & exceptions: Closed Thanksgiving and December 25 (standard Met practice; confirm on the site). Ticket valid for the printed date only.

Rules: Backpacks must be worn on the front or carried; no food/drink except a sealed water bottle; no tripods, selfie sticks or drones; non-flash photography for personal use only; large bags and luggage refused

Accessibility: Step-free entrances; mobility scooters allowed; service dogs welcome

Best for: Half day minimum — 5,000 years of art; the Temple of Dendur and the European Paintings wing alone are 2–3 hours

Hours note: The Met's Plan Your Visit page publishes tickets, transit, parking and accessibility in full but the hours module did not render for this fetch. Do not print hours you have not read — check metmuseum.org the week of travel.

The Met Cloisters hours unverified

Hours: hours not published
Admission: Same ticket structure as the Met: Adults $30, Seniors $22, Students $17, 12 & under free

Museum of Modern Art (MoMA) hours verified

11 West 53rd Street, New York, NY 10019

Hours: Mon–Thu & Sat–Sun 10:30–17:30 · Fri 10:30–20:30
Admission: Adults $30 · Seniors (65+) $22 · Visitors with disabilities $22 (care partner free) · Students $17 · 16 & under free · Members free. Tickets include all galleries and special exhibitions.

Booking: https://visit.moma.org/select

Closures & exceptions: Open every day except Thanksgiving and Christmas. Galleries close ~15 min before the Museum. UNIQLO Friday Nights: free for New York State residents 17:30–20:30, ticket must be reserved in advance. Members-only weekend mornings 09:30–10:30 in select galleries.

Rules: All bags inspected; backpacks must be checked; no large umbrellas; service animals only

Best for: 2–3 hours; pairs naturally with The Modern (same building) for a lunch-to-dinner day

Statue of Liberty & Ellis Island (ferry access only) hours verified

Liberty Island / Ellis Island — ferries from Battery Park (NY) and Liberty State Park (NJ)

Hours: Daily 09:00–16:30 (park grounds); last statue entry 15:30
Admission: National Park entrance is free; the concessioner charges for the ferry, and separately for pedestal/crown access. Ferry price not captured from an official page in this pass — UNVERIFIED, book on cityexperiences.com (the official ferry operator for NPS).

Booking: https://www.cityexperiences.com/new-york/city-cruises/statue-of-liberty/

Closures & exceptions: Closed Thanksgiving (2026-11-26) and Christmas Day. Access is by ferry only — there is no bridge. Crown access requires a separate reservation made months ahead.

Practical: Security screening at Battery Park is the bottleneck; arrive at the first ferry. February wind on the water is brutal — this is the one activity that is genuinely worse in your window than in spring.

Best for: Half day. Grounds-only (no pedestal) is the low-commitment version.

Top of the Rock Observation Deck hours verified

30 Rockefeller Plaza, New York, NY 10112

Hours: Daily 08:00–24:00 (live 'OPEN 8 AM – 12 AM' banner on the official ticket page)
Admission: Timed admission from $42 · Skip-the-line from $82 · VIP Pass from $185 (guided, priority, Beam + SKYLIFT, champagne toast)

Booking: https://www.rockefellercenter.com/buy-tickets/

Closures & exceptions: Timed-entry slots; sunset slots sell out first. VIP pass gains a Christmas-tree experience 3 Dec–3 Jan only.

Best for: The view that includes the Empire State Building (which the Empire State Building's own deck cannot give you). Go 30 min before sunset.

The High Line hours verified

Elevator/stairs access at Gansevoort St, 14th St, 23rd St, 30th St — Manhattan's West Side

Hours: 1 Dec – 31 Mar: 07:00–20:00 · 1 Apr – 30 Nov: 07:00–22:00
Admission: Free, no ticket

Closures & exceptions: The Rail Yard Preserve section (30th–34th St) closes at dusk. Free docent-led tours run on select days. Elevator outages are posted on a status page — check it if anyone in the party cannot use stairs.

Rules: No smoking, bicycles, skateboards or scooters; dogs not permitted (service dogs only); no alcohol outside authorised areas; professional photo/film shoots need a permit

Accessibility: Stairs, ramps and elevators at multiple entries; wheelchair loan must be requested at least one business day ahead (requests reviewed Mon–Fri 09:00–17:00). Citi Bike docks and bike racks at street level.

Best for: A 1.5-mile walk; enter at Gansevoort, exit at 23rd, and land in Chelsea for lunch. Cold but empty in February — actually a plus.

In your window: Your Feb window falls in the winter schedule — 07:00–20:00 daily.
