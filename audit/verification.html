Source policy

Rule 1

Official place/agency pages outrank search snippets, secondary guides, and platform search links.

Rule 2

Dynamic quote pages are snapshots with a capture date; they are not predictions or booking-date experiments.

Rule 3

YouTube oEmbed confirms platform metadata only; it does not prove review quality or venue endorsement.

Rule 4

TikTok/Instagram search and profile links are discovery-only, not verified reviews.

Rule 5

Modeled coordinates, straight-line screens, arithmetic-derived totals, and community-router samples are not official-source verification.
