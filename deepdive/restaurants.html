Restaurants

New York holds 5 three-star, 14 two-star and 50 one-star restaurants in the 2026-11-25 Michelin Guide. The 10 most relevant to a seven-night trip are below with menus, prices, hours, closures and video.

A star is not a listing. Michelin also lists 76 Bib Gourmand ("good quality, good value cooking") and 224 Selected restaurants in NYC. Those carry no star. This site keeps them in separate tables so the two are never conflated.

What the cost table means for the plan

  • Cheapest full tasting for two: $430 at Saga (6 courses). Priciest captured: $770 at Eleven Madison Park — and Per Se, Atera and Sushi Sho publish no price at all, so the true ceiling is unknown.
  • Lunch is the arbitrage. Le Bernardin's 3-course lunch is $142 against a $225 dinner; The Modern's lunch prix fixe is $115 against a $185 dinner. Two lunches instead of two dinners saves about $300 for a couple.
  • Every 2★ and 3★ dining room here requires a booking. The Modern's Bar Room (walk-ins accepted in the Bar Room, the lounge and at the marble bar — stated on themodernnyc.com), Gramercy Tavern's Tavern, Le Bernardin's lounge menu and Jungsik's bar are the four à-la-carte doors into a starred kitchen. Only The Modern's walk-in policy is published; book the other three.
