The three decisions that matter

1. Sleep: book Arlo Soho unless the room matters more than the price

Arlo Soho at $1,721.14 for seven nights is the cheapest room on the shortlist that still has a queen bed and a private bathroom — but the queen rooms there are 150–155 ft², which is tight for two people and a week of luggage. If that is a deal-breaker, The Maritime Hotel at $2,434.94 gives you a 279 ft² queen and breakfast for $713.80 more across the week. That $102 a night buys 124 additional square feet and the best guest score on the list (19.0/20).

Both quotes are live availability for 15–22 Feb 2027 pulled on 2026-11-25; re-quote before booking.

2. Eat: three starred dinners is the right number for seven nights

A starred dinner for two runs from $356 (Gramercy Tavern's seasonal menu) to $770 (Eleven Madison Park's full tasting), with Saga at $430 and Gabriel Kreuther at $400 in between. Doing that every night is exhausting and roughly $3,000 in food alone. Three tasting menus plus four relaxed nights — the Tavern at Gramercy, the Bar Room at The Modern, and two non-starred rooms — is the version of this trip people actually enjoy.

3. Move: CIVILIAN Hotel if the restaurants are the point

CIVILIAN Hotel sits at 305 W. 48th Street, New York City, NY — a measured 14-minute walk from Per Se, one C-train ride from Atera, and two blocks from Broadway. Its neighbour MADE Hotel at 44 W 29th St is the closest room on the list to Eleven Madison Park: a measured 9-minute walk. It costs $2,389.69 for the week, which is $668.55 over the cheapest option and about half what The Mercer asks for the same seven nights.

What this site does and does not claim
