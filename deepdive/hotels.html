What the price does not tell you

  • Room size is the real differentiator at the bottom of this list. Arlo Soho (150–155 ft²) and CIVILIAN (130 ft²) are genuinely small for two people and a week of luggage. SIXTY LES gives 325 ft² for $2,147 — the best square-foot-per-dollar on the list.
  • The Maritime and the Marlton both include breakfast in the quoted rate. At $2,435 for the week the Maritime's inclusion is worth roughly $250–350 against the hotels that do not include it — which roughly erases its gap to SIXTY LES.
  • The Mercer is the luxury ceiling, at 5.5× the cheapest qualifying room. It is in this table only so the ceiling is visible.
  • Refinery has no 7-night total on its page. Multiplying the nightly rate by seven would be inventing a number, so it is blank.
  • ModernHaus and The Mercer street addresses were not transcribed from their pages during this pass, so they are absent rather than approximated. See the questions register.
