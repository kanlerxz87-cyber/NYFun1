Things you should decide before booking

Eleven Madison Park is a deadline, not a choice

EMP releases reservations on the first of the month for the following month and its FAQ states that all sales are final and non-refundable. A February 2027 table opens on 1 February 2027 — the day before your flight. Booking it means committing $770 for two, non-refundable, sight unseen, at the last minute. If that is not a risk you want, the plan works without it: Saga at $215 pp or The Modern at $185 pp deliver the same "evening" at half the exposure.

Three starred dinners, or four?

The overview's shape uses three. Adding a fourth (Gabriel Kreuther, $400 for two at the 3-course, is the cheapest 2★ full dinner on the list) pushes food to roughly $2,300 for two and makes four of seven nights a 3-hour commitment. The trade-off is real and it is yours — the data supports either.

Is the Met worth a full day in February?

The Met is the best indoor answer to a cold week, and it is free-adjacent for NY residents only — you are not one, so $30 each. But it is 10 minutes from the 6-train and the whole Upper East Side is a dead zone for the starred restaurants on this list. Expect the Met day to end downtown.
