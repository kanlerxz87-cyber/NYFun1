The good news about this city's grid

  • Eight of the nine measured legs needed zero transfers. The one-transfer legs in the dataset are the exceptions, not the rule.
  • CIVILIAN → Per Se is a 14-minute walk. CIVILIAN → Atera is 35 minutes by one C-train ride. That pair of facts is the whole argument for staying in the theatre district.
  • SIXTY LES → Atera is 22 minutes for a Lower East Side hotel and a Tribeca restaurant, which is better than most people expect.

Fares

MTA subway and local bus fare is a flat $2.90 per ride (OMNY or MetroCard); a 7-day unlimited MetroCard costs $34. The fare was not re-verified from mta.info in this pass — treat the numbers as indicative until checked.

Practical

  • February 2027 NYC weather means most of these rides will feel longer than the router says — platform waits are outdoors on the bus routes that serve several of these legs.
  • Every measured leg above used zero or one transfer. The grid is forgiving: almost everything on this list is one seat away from almost everything else.
  • CIVILIAN at 305 W 48th St and MADE at 44 W 29th are the two hotels that put multiple 3★ rooms inside a 15-minute walk.
  • For late tasting-menu seatings (21:00+), check the subway line's weekend/weeknight status before committing — the C and E both drop frequency after 22:00.

What is missing here

No isochrone matrix. The transit API's one-to-all endpoint failed on every call, so this page cannot honestly say "hotel X reaches 41 starred restaurants within 60 minutes". It says what was measured and labels the cluster screen as a straight-line screen. If the matrix matters, a session with direct API access to a routing engine could produce it — see the questions register.
