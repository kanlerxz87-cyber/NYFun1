Source hierarchy applied

  1. The venue's own website or its own booking platform (Tock, Resy) — used for prices, hours, policies.
  2. The Michelin Guide's own page — used for distinctions, addresses, cuisine, price band, and hours where the venue publishes none.
  3. NPS / museum / operator official pages — used for attraction hours, admission and closures.
  4. OpenStreetMap via Nominatim — used only for coordinates, never for hours or prices.
  5. Nothing else. No aggregators, no review sites, no video descriptions, no memory.
