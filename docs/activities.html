Tickets: what to buy in advance

Must pre-book (timed entry)

  • 9/11 Memorial & Museum — timed entry required; open Wed–Mon 09:00–19:00 (last entry 18:00).
  • Top of the Rock — timed sunset slots cost more than the $42 floor.
  • Statue of Liberty — Statue City Cruises is the sole NPS operator; Crown/Pedestal ($0.30) books out weeks early.
  • MoMA & AMNH — timed tickets live through mid-2027; recommended during NYCPS Midwinter Recess (15–19 Feb 2027).

Can be decided on the day

  • The Met Fifth Avenue — Sun–Tue & Thu 10–17, Fri–Sat 10–21, closed Wednesdays.
  • Central Park, High Line, Brooklyn Bridge — free entry, no tickets.
  • Chelsea Market — free entry (07:00–22:00), pay each food vendor.
  • Broadway — choose show first on broadway.org; most shows are dark Mondays.

The one-hour rule, applied from 538 W 58th St

Measured from Fairfield Central Park's verified building coordinates (40.77042, -73.98971):

Walkable in 7–20 minutes

Central Park SW entrance (0.6 km / 7 min walk), Broadway theatres (1.34 km / 18 min walk), Top of the Rock (1.52 km / 20 min walk), MoMA (1.43 km / 19 min walk).

17–25 minutes by subway

AMNH (1.79 km / 17 min transit), The Met (2.45 km / 20 min transit via crosstown or subway), The High Line (2.80 km / 22 min), Chelsea Market (3.41 km / 25 min).

42–44 minutes by express subway

9/11 Memorial (6.85 km / 42 min modelled via A/C express from Columbus Circle), Brooklyn Bridge (7.18 km / 44 min). Plan as half-day outings.

Marginally past 60 min: Statue of Liberty

Battery Park Ferry Terminal is 7.82 km (~47 modelled subway min), plus the ~15–20 min harbor ferry = ~62–67 min door-to-island. High winter winds can suspend sailings, so keep Sunday's buffer ready (Q-15).
