Why the activity list is short

The brief fixes the trip in February. Several headline NYC activities are outdoor and weather-exposed (Brooklyn Bridge walkway, the High Line, Statue of Liberty ferry, Central Park). A February plan should weight indoor options and keep one outdoor slot flexible. That is a planning judgement, not a verified fact — but it is why the itinerary leads with museums and buildings and treats walks as the "if it is above 5 °C" option.

What "verified" means per row

  • VERIFIED — hours, price and closures read directly off the official site on 2026-11-25.
  • PARTIAL — some fields off the official site, others only from independent secondary guides; the row says which.
  • UNVERIFIED — official page did not yield the field and no reliable substitute was accepted. Blank.
