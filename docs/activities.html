Tickets and schedules to verify

Timed tickets or advance booking

  • 9/11 Memorial & Museum — timed tickets are required; last entry is 60 minutes before closing.
  • Top of the Rock — timed admission; the official price varies by date and time.
  • Statue of Liberty — ferry is mandatory and only Statue City Cruises may dock at the islands. February 2027 sailings were not present in the schedule checked.
  • MoMA and AMNH — reserve ahead for faster entry; MoMA Friday Nights' free NY State resident offer requires advance reservation.

Can be decided closer to the day

  • The Met — standard weekly hours and adult admission are captured; recheck temporary notices.
  • Central Park, High Line, Brooklyn Bridge — free public access, subject to closures and conditions.
  • Chelsea Market — free entry; vendors set their own hours, including on holidays.
  • Broadway — no single official price; select a show first (Q-16).

Modelled reach is not measured reach

The simple model puts 10 of 11 activities under 60 minutes. This is a straight-line/speed approximation, not an MTA-scheduled door-to-door measurement.

Statue of Liberty is not counted as under an hour. The former 56-minute result applied a generic land-transit speed to a straight-line distance ending at Liberty Island. That is not a valid route: the island requires a ferry, and the February 2027 sailing schedule, crossing, boarding, and waiting time are not verified. Treat it as an optional half-day whose timing remains unknown (F-20, Q-15, Q-19).
For the other ten activities: displayed minutes are provisional estimates from area-level coordinates, a fixed speed, and an access/wait allowance. They do not prove that the one-hour requirement is met in practice (F-05).
