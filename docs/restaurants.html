MICHELIN restaurants

All 72 star entries from MICHELIN's All the Stars in The MICHELIN Guide to New York City 2025, published 18 November 2025. The badge in column two is the distinction: stars are awards, and a venue with no star icon on MICHELIN's site is listed, not starred.

This list changes on 14 December 2026 — before the trip. The next New York ceremony is that day, so any star shown here may move. Re-read the MICHELIN ceremony page after that date (flag F-01).
Read the two middle columns carefully: most of them are empty, on purpose. Only 2 of the 72 star restaurants published hours that could be read, and only 3 published a menu price. The rest of the roster is complete on name, distinction, cuisine, borough and official link — and blank on hours and price, because the alternative would be inventing them. The counts are on the repo review page and the way to close each gap is in Q-06 and Q-09.
Two official MICHELIN pages disagree. The ceremony article lists 72 entries (5 / 15 / 52). MICHELIN's own filtered list pages, read the same day, report 69 (5 / 14 / 50). This table publishes the 72 ceremony entries and flags the discrepancy rather than picking a winner (F-02, question Q-17).

Read this before using "cluster" to pick a hotel. Of the 72 entries, coordinates were verified for only 7. So the borough grouping above covers all 72, while the distances table covers a 7-venue sample.
The data does not support a "star cluster" you can book around. The 7 venues with verified coordinates do not sit close together: only Eleven Madison Park and Gramercy Tavern are within a kilometre of anything else (353 m apart). Le Bernardin, Per Se, Sushi Sho, Daniel and Jungsik are each more than a kilometre from the next scored venue. That is the opposite of a cluster — it means star dining in Manhattan is dispersed, and the useful question is not "which cluster?" but "which hotel is within an hour of the specific restaurant I booked?" The answer for all seven shortlisted hotels is in the MICHELIN reach ranking.
What would make a real cluster analysis possible: verified coordinates for all 72 entries, then a density calculation. That needs one page read per restaurant and is the same work as closing F-05. Nothing on this site pretends to have done it.
