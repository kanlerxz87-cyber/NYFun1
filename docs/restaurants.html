What the clusters actually look like

  • Midtown / Midtown East holds the densest verified cluster: Le Bernardin, Per Se, Sushi Sho, Aquavit — all reachable in 8–17 modelled minutes from the recommended hotel.
  • Tribeca / Lower Manhattan (Jungsik, and the bulk of the one-star roster) is a 20–30 minute subway ride — inside the one-hour rule but not walkable in February.
  • Brooklyn (Aska, Francie, Oxomoco, Restaurant Yuu, Shota Omakase, The Four Horsemen) is reachable within the hour from Midtown by subway; modelling it precisely needs addresses this pass did not capture — see Q9.
  • Outside the one-hour rule: Blue Hill at Stone Barns (Tarrytown) and La Bastide by Andrea Calstier (North Salem). Both need a car or Metro-North and a half-day. Excluded by design.
