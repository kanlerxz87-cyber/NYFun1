What is deliberately missing

  • Airfare — origin airport unknown (Q11). Nothing here assumes a fare.
  • Subway/bus fares — the MTA fare for February 2027 is not published in a form we could verify in this pass; we refuse to quote a 2026 fare as a 2027 cost. Flag F19.
  • Menu prices — no restaurant's tasting-menu price was captured from its own site. The spend column is a band-based model (S5).
  • Travel insurance — depends on the refundable/non-refundable decision (Q6).
Price-sensitivity finding: shifting from the 15–22 February window to 8–15 March added about $1,204 to the same room for the same week. Date choice moves the budget more than hotel choice does.
