Suggestions for improvement

12 concrete improvements to this site, each written so it can be accepted or rejected on its own. The point of this page is that you review the options and choose a path before anything is implemented.

How to use it. Read the problem, then the proposal, then the effort. The decision dropdown is local to your browser and changes nothing on the site — it is a worksheet. Anything you mark "Do it" can be implemented in the order shown; S-01 and S-02 should come first because they affect the correctness of everything else.

Decision

If you only do three

1 · S-02 Measured travel times

Turns the one-hour rule from a model into a measurement. Highest impact on the hotel ranking.

2 · S-01 One canonical site

Removes the second dataset that contradicts this one on stars, hotels and transit.

3 · S-04 Finish the menu prices

Makes the dining budget complete instead of partial.

What I deliberately did not propose

  • Merging the two datasets into one table. They were researched independently and disagree; merging would silently pick a winner. Better to archive one (S-01).
  • Filling blank prices with "typical" values. It would make the site look complete and be worthless.
  • Guessing social handles for venues that publish none. A search link is honest; an invented handle is not.
