Itinerary

Three concrete variants, so switching length is a choice rather than a re-plan. The 7-night version is the recommendation; the 4-night version is the literal preferred window; the 14-night version is the upper end of the brief.

Dinner placement logic. Le Bernardin is closed on Sundays and does not serve lunch on Saturdays. The 9/11 Museum opens Wednesday–Monday. MoMA opens at 10:30 every day and runs late only on Fridays. Those three facts drive the order of days below.

Booking order that matters

  1. Hotel first, on a free-cancellation rate — the deadline on the recommended rate is 12 February 2027, so nothing is at risk while you decide.
  2. Starred dinners roughly 30 days out. Most New York tasting menus release reservations about a month ahead. This 30-day pattern is a common practice, not a rule I verified per venue — check each one.
  3. Timed tickets (9/11 Museum, Top of the Rock) once the dates are fixed; they sell out on holiday weekends.
  4. Statue of Liberty ferry before the trip; it is the only way onto the island.
  5. Broadway only after choosing a show.

February reality check

The itinerary includes outdoor walking (Brooklyn Bridge, High Line, Central Park) in February. No official climate data was captured for this site, so no temperature figure is quoted here — that is deliberate (see suggestion S-10). Plan for a cold-weather wardrobe and keep a buffer day, which is why day 7 is deliberately unscheduled.
