Itinerary

Three concrete variants (7-night recommended, 3-night / 4-day preferred window, 14-night full window) engineered around actual museum closure days, winter hours, holiday crowds, and MICHELIN dinner schedules.

Scheduling rules enforced:
  • The Met is CLOSED every Wednesday — placed on Tue 16 Feb across both the 7-night and 4-night plans (never Wed 17 Feb!).
  • 9/11 Museum is open Wed–Mon (last entry 18:00; closed most Tuesdays) — scheduled Thu 18 Feb (or Wed 17 on 4-night).
  • Most Broadway shows are dark Monday — Mon 15 Feb arrival stays flexible.
  • Sunday-closed vs Sunday-open stars: Le Bernardin, Gabriel Kreuther, Jean-Georges, Atomix, Aquavit & Dirt Candy close Sundays; EMP, Jungsik, Per Se, Sushi Sho, The Modern, Saga, Gramercy Tavern, Crown Shy & Cote all serve Sundays.

February weather & packing (NWS Central Park normals)

Verified against NWS 1991–2020 NYC Central Park Climate Normals:

  • February average: Daily high 42.2°F (5.7°C), overnight low 29.5°F (-1.4°C), 10.1 in mean snowfall (3.19 in precip).
  • March average (for 14-night or late options): High 49.9°F (9.9°C), low 35.8°F (2.1°C), 5.0 in snow.
  • Packing & plan implications: Exposed outdoor legs (Brooklyn Bridge, Statue ferry deck, High Line) require thermal base layers, windproof coat, gloves, hat, and waterproof walking shoes. Day 7 (Sun 21 Feb) remains a deliberate indoor swap buffer.
