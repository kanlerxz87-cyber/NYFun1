Itinerary

Three concrete variants, so changing the length is a choice rather than a re-plan. The 7-night version is the recommendation; the literal preferred window is 3 hotel nights (15–18 Feb); the 14-night version is the upper end of the brief.

Opening-day logic. The Met is closed Wednesdays and otherwise follows the captured weekly schedule; AMNH is open daily 10:00–17:30. The 9/11 Museum opens Wednesday–Monday, with select Tuesday openings. February 2027 Statue City Cruises sailings were not available in the schedule checked, so that visit remains optional rather than a fixed timed stop.

Booking order that matters

  1. Hotel first, if the chosen rate and full checkout breakdown are acceptable. The current Fairfield King Room snapshot allows free cancellation until 12 February 2027; recheck the terms before paying.
  2. Starred dinners when each booking window opens. Release windows differ by venue; check the selected restaurant's official booking platform rather than assuming one rule.
  3. Timed tickets (9/11 Museum, Top of the Rock) once the dates are fixed; check release dates and availability.
  4. Statue of Liberty ferry only after February 2027 sailings are published; the ferry is the only way onto the island.
  5. Broadway only after choosing a show.

February reality check

The itinerary includes outdoor walking (Brooklyn Bridge, High Line, Central Park) in February. No official climate data was captured for this site, so no temperature figure is quoted here (S-10).
