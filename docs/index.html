Scope & Method

  • Official-first verification: Michelin star status, restaurant address/hours/website were checked on guide.michelin.com restaurant pages (accessed 2026-11-25). Hotel amenity claims were checked on official hotel pages where accessible.
  • No fabricated pricing: Any hotel/restaurant price not confirmed from an official or clearly cited source is left blank and marked Unverified.
  • Transit estimates: Travel-time claims are estimates based on typical MTA subway/bus routing; they are flagged as estimates unless backed by a specific official trip planner result. Users should verify on MTA / Google Maps at travel time.
  • Social/video links: Provided as discovery links (official YouTube/Instagram/TikTok or search queries) where direct official social links were not confirmed; each is labeled.
  • Coordination: See COORDINATION.md in repo root for task ownership and status.
