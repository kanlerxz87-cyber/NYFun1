Executive summary

1 · The decision

Book Fairfield Inn & Suites by Marriott New York Manhattan/Central Park, King Room, for 15–22 February 2027 at $985.60 before taxes ($1,158.98 derived all-in), on the free-cancellation rate (cancel free until 12 Feb 2027). Once tax is included it is the cheapest of the seven, it includes breakfast for two, the bed is a king and the bathroom is attached.

Every hotel total on Booking.com is printed before tax. Arlo SoHo advertises $1,021.00 against Fairfield's $985.60, but on the derived all-in basis Fairfield is $1,158.98 and Arlo SoHo is $1,206.60. The 25 November figure of $1,155.48 for Fairfield was withdrawn after a line-by-line re-read of its source (F-17, F-23); the ranking uses the corrected all-in column and discloses the promotion (F-03).

2 · Why 7 nights, not 3

The brief's preferred window is 15–18 February (3 nights) but it also asks for a 1–2 week trip. Those two statements cannot both be satisfied, so the plan uses 7 nights and keeps a 4-night variant and a 14-night variant on the Itinerary page as choices. No re-planning is needed to switch.

15 February 2027 is Washington's Birthday on OPM's official 2027 holiday list. Holiday demand and thinner restaurant availability are plausible but not measured.

3 · The restaurants

MICHELIN's 18 November 2025 ceremony lists 72 star entries: 5 three-star, 15 two-star and 52 one-star, and 3 of the star holders also carry a Green Star. 63 are in Manhattan, 6 in Brooklyn, 1 in Queens and 2 outside the city (Tarrytown and North Salem — not reachable within the hour).

Only Le Bernardin ($350 per person) and Gabriel Kreuther ($325 per person) published a menu price that could be read. Five more priority venues are blank rather than guessed. See the table →

4 · The one-hour rule

All seven shortlisted hotels are inside the modelled one-hour reach of the verified star restaurants, so the rule does not separate them — price and breakfast do. That is a model result, not a measurement, and it is labelled as such throughout (flag F-05).

A measured travel-time matrix is the single highest-value improvement available: suggestion S-02.

5 · What is verified, and what is not

  • $4,184.98 priced subtotal across hotel, flights, transit, six tickets and two tasting menus; the hotel line is derived and ordinary meals are blank.
  • 2 of 72 restaurants have verified hours and 4 have a verified menu price. The other 66–70 rows are complete on name, distinction, cuisine, borough and official link — and deliberately blank on hours and price (see the table).
  • 11 verified official social accounts and 11 verified third-party review videos across 7 venues, each opened and identified by title, channel and view count. Where nothing could be verified the cell is still a search link, labelled as a search — TikTok returned HTTP 400 on every read, so no TikTok account is claimed anywhere.
  • 23 flagged items and 27 open questions, each with the exact source that would settle it.

6 · The three things that could invalidate this plan

  1. 14 December 2026: MICHELIN publishes a new New York list. Stars can move before you travel.
  2. Hotel prices are live: every total here is a 25 Nov 2026 snapshot and several depend on a time-limited promotion.
  3. Menu prices are mostly unknown: four priority restaurants publish no readable price, so the food budget is incomplete by design rather than by accident.
