Executive summary

1 · The recommendation

For 15–22 February 2027, Fairfield is the lowest displayed base room rate and has the lowest derived total among these seven sampled offers: a King Room, private bathroom, breakfast included, and free cancellation until 12 February. Booking.com showed $985.60 before taxes and excluded fees. Adding the displayed 14.75% tax and $4/night line gives a derived estimate of $1,158.98; the official fixed-charge calculation gives $1,155.48. Neither is a final checkout quote; Booking.com's fixed fee differs from the official NYC/NYS components by $0.50 per night (F-04).

Fairfield is the lowest displayed pre-tax room rate among these seven on the 2026-11-26 snapshot. Compare the exact room and checkout breakdown on the Hotels page before booking.

2 · Why seven nights, not three?

The preferred window, 15–18 February, is three hotel nights across four calendar dates. The 7-night plan is the one that meets the brief's 1–2-week requirement; the exact 3-night preferred-date and 14-night alternatives are shown on the Itinerary page.

OPM confirms Monday 15 February 2027 is Washington's Birthday. That establishes the federal holiday date, not a hotel-demand or price effect (F-11).

3 · The MICHELIN list is dated

The 2025 ceremony article contains 72 entries (5 three-star, 15 two-star, 52 one-star). The live official star filters read 2026-11-26 show 69 (5/14/50). MICHELIN scheduled the next Northeast Cities ceremony, including New York, for 14 December 2026, before the trip.

Le Bernardin and Gabriel Kreuther have readable tasting-menu prices; Per Se's deposit floor is labelled separately. See the dated roster and source notes →

4 · The one-hour rule is not yet measured

Hotel reach is shown only as a straight-line 5 km coordinate proxy; activity minutes are simple speed models. Neither is an MTA itinerary or a measured door-to-door time. Liberty Island requires a ferry; the February 2027 sailing schedule, boarding, waiting, and full journey time are not verified. Do not treat the Statue of Liberty as under an hour (F-05, F-20).

A measured route matrix, including separate ferry legs, remains the highest-value travel check (Q-19).

5 · What is verified, and what is not

  • 2 of 72 restaurants have captured hours and 3 have a readable menu-price value. The rest remain roster-only on those fields.
  • 10 directly verified official social accounts, 1 unconfirmed Instagram reference, and 3 third-party review videos are linked; platform searches are labelled, and no TikTok account is asserted.
  • 10 of 11 activities have a sub-hour result in the simple model; this is not a measured route guarantee.
  • 21 review records (16 open) and 26 tracked questions are itemized.

6 · Three things that could change the plan

  1. 14 December 2026: MICHELIN publishes the new Northeast Cities selection; restaurant distinctions may change.
  2. Hotel checkout charges: Booking.com's $4/$6 nightly fixed-fee labels differ from the $3.50 statutory NYC/NYS fixed components.
  3. Measured travel: no full MTA route matrix or February 2027 ferry schedule is recorded yet.
