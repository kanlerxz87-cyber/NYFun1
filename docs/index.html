Executive summary

1 · The decision

Book Fairfield Inn & Suites by Marriott New York Manhattan/Central Park (538 W 58th St, King Room) for 15–22 February 2027 at $985.60 pre-tax / $1,155.48 all-in as printed by Booking.com (incl. taxes & fees, read 2026-11-27) on the free-cancellation rate ($0 prepayment, cancel free until 12 Feb 2027). It is the cheapest all-in option of all seven shortlisted hotels, charges zero resort fee, includes American breakfast for two, and puts Columbus Circle express lines (A/B/C/D/1) a block away.

Why parity matters: Booking.com's 2026-11-27 room tables now print tax-inclusive totals directly; the official NYC rate is 14.75% + $3.50/night fixed fees ($2.00 room fee + $1.50 NYS unit fee, verified on nyc.gov). Even Pestana's non-refundable Partner Offer ($1,271.00 all-in, no breakfast) and Arlo SoHo's non-refundable Black Friday rate ($1,497.39 all-in with a $37/nt resort fee, no breakfast) cost more than Fairfield's refundable, breakfast-included total. Next-best free-cancel pick with breakfast is Hyatt Place Chelsea ($1,303.00 all-in, #1 reach at 22/22 stars within 5 km).

The brief asks for a 1–2 week stay between 15 Feb and 23 Mar 2027 while also naming 15–18 Feb as preferred. 15–22 February satisfies both and tested as the cheapest of all five weekly Mon–Mon windows across the entire brief range (Week 2 rises +3.6%; March weeks jump +26% to +39%). 4-night and 14-night variants remain ready on the Itinerary page.
