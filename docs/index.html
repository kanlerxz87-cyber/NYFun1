Executive summary

1. What this trip is

A 7-night, 2-adult New York City trip, Monday 15 February → Monday 22 February 2027, built around MICHELIN-starred dining and short transit hops. 15 February is the exact start date the brief preferred; we extended the preferred 15–18 February window to 7 nights because the brief also requires a 1–2 week trip. That conflict is Open Question Q1 — the itinerary page also contains a 4-night and a 14-night variant so the decision is a choice, not a re-plan.

Calendar note: 15 February 2027 is the third Monday of February, i.e. the US federal Presidents' Day holiday, and the start of the week many NYC schools are on midwinter recess. Expect holiday demand. The school-recess claim is not verified — see flag F16.

2. The one decision that matters most

Book Fairfield Inn & Suites by Marriott New York Manhattan/Central Park on a free-cancellation rate now, then re-price weekly. It is the cheapest of the four hotels we could price with a verified queen-or-bigger bed and a private bathroom — $985.60 for 7 nights before tax ($1,159 estimated all-in) — and it still puts all 6 verified MICHELIN restaurants inside 30 minutes (MODEL).

If location prestige matters more than price, citizenM New York Times Square (#2, est. $1,344 all-in) is a ~0.4 km walk from Le Bernardin and Per Se and has the highest guest score of the shortlist (8.9/10).

3. MICHELIN picture

The current selection is the one published at the 18 November 2025 ceremony: 5 three-star, 15 two-star and 52 one-star entries (72 total), plus 4 Green Stars. 63 star entries are in Manhattan, 6 in Brooklyn, 1 in Queens, and 2 outside NYC (Tarrytown and North Salem — outside a 1-hour NYC transit trip).

Star status is a moving target. The next New York ceremony is reported for 14 December 2026 — i.e. after this dataset and before the trip. Star counts must be re-verified in January 2027. Flag F11.

4. Timing

Of the three 7-night windows we could price for the same hotel and room, 15–22 February is the cheapest: $1,147 vs $1,288 (22 Feb–1 Mar) and $2,351 (8–15 Mar, where the cheapest available 2-guest room was a different, dearer room type). That is a 105% swing for the same hotel — so travelling in February is not just a preference, it is worth roughly $1,200 on a 7-night stay.

Sample size: one hotel, three windows. Treat it as direction, not a full calendar. Flag F10.
