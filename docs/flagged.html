F-04 MEDIUM
All seven hotel totals exclude tax; the per-night city tax is not uniform
Every Booking.com room row used here prints "Total before taxes" and lists 14.75% tax as excluded. The city tax is $4/night at most properties but $6/night at Residence Inn FiDi. Arlo SoHo and Arlo Midtown also exclude a $1/night tourism fee. The site derives an all-in column per hotel from the fees printed on that hotel's own row.official pageRead the 'Excluded' line on each room row (done) and apply tax where excluded
F-05 HIGH
The one-hour MICHELIN rule is modelled, not measured
Travel times on this site are computed from straight-line distance at an assumed average speed, then labelled MODEL. Only a handful of legs were checked against a real transit router. A model can be wrong in either direction, especially across the East River where there is no direct subway.official pageRun each hotel-to-restaurant pair through an MTA-scheduled journey planner for a weekday 19:00 departure

F-18 HIGH
A 'star cluster' cannot be demonstrated from this dataset
Coordinates were verified for only 7 of the 72 star entries. Among those 7, only Eleven Madison Park and Gramercy Tavern sit within a kilometre of another scored venue (353 m). The others are each more than 1 km from the next one, so Manhattan star dining is dispersed rather than clustered. Any neighbourhood-level cluster map would be invented.official pageVerify coordinates for all 72 entries and compute density
F-19 MEDIUM
The hotel reach ranking covers 7 restaurants, not 72
The brief asks hotels to be ranked by how many MICHELIN restaurants are reachable within an hour. Only the 7 venues with verified coordinates can be scored, so the ranking is out of 7 rather than out of 72. The five Midtown and Chelsea hotels are effectively tied on it, which is why cost decides the recommendation.official pageVerify all 72 coordinates and run a measured journey matrix
F-20 MEDIUM
Activity travel times are modelled, and the Statue of Liberty only just qualifies

It is the one activity where the rule is satisfied by a margin thin enough that a real schedule could break it.official pageQuery a scheduled journey planner for the hotel-to-Battery-Park leg at 09:00 on a weekday

The four that matter most

  1. F-01 — the MICHELIN list changes on 14 December 2026, before the trip.
  2. F-02 — MICHELIN's own ceremony article (72) and live filter (69) disagree.
  3. F-05 — the one-hour travel rule is modelled, not measured.
  4. F-03 / F-04 — hotel prices are live snapshots, and some totals exclude tax.

What this register is for

Each flag names the source that would close it. Closing all of them is a bounded amount of work: most are a single page read. The ones that are not — F-01 and F-02 — depend on MICHELIN publishing, and cannot be closed early.

Built 2026-11-25. Every figure on this site was read on an official page on that date unless the row says otherwise. Prices and hours change; re-check before booking. Rows that could not be verified are blank and listed on Flagged.
