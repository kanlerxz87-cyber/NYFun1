F-04 HIGH
Booking.com fixed nightly charges conflict with the statutory NYC/NYS components
Open
Booking.com shows a $4 City tax per night for six hotels and $6/night for Residence Inn FiDi. NYC Finance lists a $2/night fixed hotel-occupancy tax for rooms at $40+, and NYS lists a separate $1.50/night NYC unit fee: $3.50/night combined. Both derived calculations are shown; their difference remains unresolved and neither is a checkout quote.sourceCompare the final property/Booking.com checkout breakdown with the itemized legal taxes and fees; ask the property what the $4/$6 line includes.
F-05 HIGH
The one-hour MICHELIN rule is modelled, not measured
Open
Displayed hotel/activity times are simple straight-line/speed models, not MTA-scheduled door-to-door journeys. No complete route matrix was measured in the official MTA planner; the router reads attempted in this audit did not return a usable itinerary. Crossing the East River, access, transfers, and service disruptions can change actual times.sourceMeasure each relevant hotel-to-restaurant/activity pair using the official MTA trip-planning tools for a stated departure time; record route, query date, and door-to-door time.
