Q1 · How long is the trip - 4, 7 or 14 nights?

Q2 · What is the all-in budget ceiling for two adults, excluding airfare?
Open - needs your decision owner: travellerblocks: hotel tier, number of starred dinners

What we know: We can price a 7-night hotel from about $1,159 (all-in, breakfast included) to $1,688 (Moxy, resort fee included). Two starred dinners for two adults model at $500-900 per dinner.

What we do not know: The ceiling, and whether airfare/transit sit inside it.

How to close it: Give a single number. If it is under $3,000 the plan must drop one starred dinner; if it is over $6,000 the shortlist should be widened upward (Q9) rather than optimised for price.

Q3 · Is 60 minutes door-to-door the hard limit, and does winter walking count against it?
Answered - 60 min stated owner: travellerblocks: restaurant shortlist

What we know: The brief sets one hour by subway or bus. All four hotels reach all six fully verified starred restaurants in 8-26 modelled minutes, so the constraint binds only for Brooklyn/Queens.

What we do not know: Whether a 15-minute walk in February wind chill is acceptable as part of that hour.

How to close it: If not, restrict dinners to the Midtown cluster (Le Bernardin, Per Se, Sushi Sho, Aquavit) and treat Tribeca/Brooklyn as taxi nights.

Q4 · How many MICHELIN-starred dinners per week is the right number?
Open - needs your decision owner: travellerblocks: food budget, itinerary
