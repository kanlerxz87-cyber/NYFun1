Required Ranking Logic

  1. Hotel candidates must meet: 2 adults, private bathroom, queen bed or equivalent/bigger.
  2. Restaurant universe: Michelin-starred and Michelin Guide-listed/selected restaurants; distinguish starred vs listed.
  3. Cluster metric: count Michelin restaurants reachable within ≤ 60 minutes by subway or bus from hotel (door-to-door estimate, documented assumptions).
  4. Ranking: primary = Michelin density within 1 hour; secondary = verified total 7-night price; tertiary = room compliance confidence and transit convenience.
  5. Verification: every address, travel time, and price must have an official/traceable source or be flagged unverified.
