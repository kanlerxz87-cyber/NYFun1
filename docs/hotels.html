What the price test showed

The recommended 15–22 Feb rate is the only one of the four sampled windows whose printed total already includes tax; the other priced windows are Black Friday rates on accessible king rooms. Their derived all-in equivalents are $1,171.02 (16–23 Feb) and $1,199.14 (22 Feb – 1 Mar).

The recommended window is the cheapest of the three priced windows — but by only about $16–$44, which is inside the noise of a live price feed. The honest conclusion is that mid-February is competitive, not dramatically cheaper. A proper "cheapest time to book" answer needs a per-night price curve across the whole 15 Feb – 23 Mar window, which is not captured (flag F-12, question Q-22).

How to read it

  • Stars within 5 km is a straight-line proxy for the one-hour rule. Manhattan walking is roughly 1.4 km per 20 minutes, so 5 km is a deliberately generous outer bound: anything inside it is certainly within an hour on foot or by a short ride, and anything outside it needs a measured trip.
  • Three-star within 5 km is the number that matters for a splurge dinner, because those are the meals worth travelling for.
  • What the rank shows: the five Midtown/Chelsea hotels are effectively tied on reach (6–7 of the 7 scored venues, 4–5 of the five three-star ones). Only Residence Inn FiDi is penalised, at 3 of 7 and 2 of 5. Since reach does not separate the other six, cost and breakfast decide it — which is why the recommendation is Fairfield.

What we could not verify about these hotels

  • Street addresses for six of seven hotels were not in the captured text (F-13), so their map coordinates are area-level and any travel time from them is softer than it looks.
  • Two cheapest qualifying rooms are accessible room types (Hyatt Place Chelsea, citizenM Bowery) — bookable by anyone, but a standard room may price differently (F-14).
  • Promotions: several totals rely on a "Black Friday Deal" valid for select stays between 19 Nov 2026 and 31 Dec 2027 (F-03). The pre-discount price is shown wherever Booking.com published one.
  • The all-in column is arithmetic, not a quote. The 14.75% rate and the $4 city tax were read on Booking.com's excluded-charges line, but the final bill can carry extra local fees that no page in this research named.
