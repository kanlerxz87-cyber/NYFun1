What the snapshots do and do not establish

What they establish: Fairfield has the lowest dated base room rate and the lowest derived total in this seven-hotel snapshot. Resort fees identified by Booking.com are already included in the relevant room rate; separately disclosed taxes and fixed charges are added in the derived estimates. The unresolved $4/$6 versus $3.50 nightly fixed-fee difference is not silently reconciled.
  • Live rates: all prices can change. Non-refundable and refundable offers are not interchangeable.
  • Fees: Booking.com displays a $4/night city tax on six properties and $6/night on Residence Inn FiDi; its fixed amounts conflict with the $3.50/night NYC/NYS components calculated from official sources. Arlo properties also list a $1/night tourism fee.
  • Coordinates: exact street addresses are now recorded, but hotel points used for the current proximity proxy have not been re-geocoded (F-13).
  • Room variants: the selected Arlo SoHo rate is labelled ADA; the displayed rates and cancellation terms differ by offer (F-14).
  • Longer stay: a separate 22 Feb–1 Mar week is sampled, but there is no combined 14-night checkout quote (F-12).
