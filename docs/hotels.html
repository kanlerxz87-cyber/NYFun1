When to book — what we can and cannot say

What we can say (verified): the rates captured on 2026-11-25 for 15–22 February 2027 are available now, are cancellable free until 12–13 February 2027, and require no prepayment. That means the low-risk move is to hold one of them now and keep shopping — a held free-cancellation room costs nothing while you watch the price.

What we cannot say: whether prices will be lower in three weeks. "Best time to book" needs a price history for these exact dates, and this session produced a single snapshot. Any statement about the ideal booking lead time would be a guess, so there is none on this page. Suggestion S3 is the mechanism to answer it properly: capture the same four URLs weekly and compare.

Why these four and not others

Booking.com returned 690 properties for the 15–22 February 2027 window (2 adults, 1 room) when we ran the search — the shortlist is deliberately four, chosen to give one option per price tier and per dining cluster, all with the bed and bathroom requirement verified. It is a shortlist, not a market survey: flag F17 and suggestion S3 explain how to widen it with a price tracker.

  • Cheapest verified: Fairfield Inn & Suites by Marriott New York Manhattan/Central Park — $985.60 pre-tax, breakfast included.
  • Best guest score: citizenM New York Times Square (8.9/10) and citizenM Bowery — compact rooms, 1 king bed, private bathroom.
  • Only option with a $35/night resort fee: Moxy NYC Times Square — it is inside the listed nightly rate but must be included in any comparison.
  • Not priced in this pass: Brooklyn and Upper East/West Side properties (address verification pending) — see Q9 and S3.
